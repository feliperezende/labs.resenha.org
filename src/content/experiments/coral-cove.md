# coral-cove

## Hypothesis (draft)

Can a social island-life game for Roblox be **original and bilingual (PT-BR + EN)** and have its entire world generated by script at startup — no external assets, no meshes, no uploads?

## Stack & constraints

- **Luau + Rojo** (`coral-cove.rbxlx` is generated by `rojo build`), on PC and mobile.
- The repo is the **implementation** of the design doc (GDD v0.5, 6 nov 2026) — decisions live in the document, rules live in Luau.
- **Zero external assets**: the whole island is built by script at start. Open the place, hit Play.
- Bilingual by design: **PT-BR + EN**.

## What shipped

- MVP v0.1 published on Roblox: universe `10775052461`, place `112005388183592`, genre **Simulation**, listing description published through the API.
- Core loop: island, gathering, fishing, farming, crafting, orders, house, and a **playable tutorial** — Tomé guides the first session (shells → delivery → reward).
- Public listing on Roblox (PT-BR description): arrive at a small island, get a simple house, help transform the village — in short, relaxing sessions.
- The island itself: shrunk from the first draft, with the giant slabs replaced by Roblox **Terrain**.
- `scripts/` for build/publish/diagnostics and a `tests/` suite alongside the Luau sources.

## Retrospective (draft)

- Shipping the MVP meant publishing through the API as well: description, genre and the experience record are code in `store/`, not fields typed into a dashboard.
